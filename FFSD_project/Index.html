About EventFlow

EventFlow is a comprehensive event management and attendee registration platform designed to simplify every aspect of event planning and execution.

Our Mission: To empower event organizers, clients, and attendees with intelligent tools that make event management seamless, secure, and delightful.

What We Offer:

  • ✨ Smart registration with instant QR ticket generation
  • 🎯 Staff coordination and assignment management
  • ✅ Live verification and on-site check-in
  • 💰 Secure payment processing
  • 📊 Real-time analytics and reporting

Founded in 2026, EventFlow has grown to manage 2,400+ events with 98,000+ attendees and 500+ organizers worldwide.
