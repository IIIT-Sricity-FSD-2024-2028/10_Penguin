Core Workflows
Workflow 1: Event Planning and Approval
Trigger: Client submits event requirements


Steps:


Client briefing


Ideation and planning by event team


Preparation of event plan and cost structure


Plan shared with client


Client approves or suggests modifications


Outcome: Approved event plan ready for execution


Workflow 2: Vendor and Staff Coordination
Trigger: Event plan approval


Steps:


Vendor selection based on scale, budget, and quality


Staff and volunteer assignment based on zones


Coordination among operations and production teams


Outcome: Resources prepared for event execution


Workflow 3: Event Execution and Monitoring
Trigger: Event day begins


Steps:


Crowd and entry management


Stage and activity coordination


Handling unexpected situations (weather, crowd surges)


Outcome: Successful completion of the event
